## Headline results

- Best mid-range operating point: sq_r16_b8 — 15.0× SH-only compression
  (205.7 MB → 13.7 MB), 34.63 dB / SSIM 0.992 / LPIPS 0.018 vs baseline renders;
  −0.62 dB vs the baseline's own GT fit. Whole model 265.7 MB → 73.7 MB (3.6×).
- Extreme compression: vq_r8_k64 — 264× SH-only, renders remain coherent
  (SSIM 0.912 vs baseline) with visible view-dependent colour desaturation.
- Honest finding: VQ quality is bounded by ⌈log2 K⌉ bits/Gaussian, not by rank;
  increasing rank beyond 8 under VQ is wasted (r16/K256 ≈ r8/K256).
- fp16 factors are free (identical metrics to fp32 at half the bytes).
